# Data slice: user stories, acceptance criteria and test cases

Owner: Sushma (Data). Every story below is covered by automated tests that run in CI on each
pull request (`make test`). The test file is named in the last column.

## User stories

| ID | As a... | I want... | so that... | Acceptance criteria |
|----|---------|-----------|------------|---------------------|
| DS-1 | traveler | the plan to use real places in the city I typed | the trip is for my destination | A Rome request schedules only Rome places. A request with no city name gets a 422. Any other city is fetched live (DS-9). No silent fallback to Lisbon. |
| DS-2 | traveler | to know which hours and prices are verified | I know what to double check before going | Every place carries `known`, `inferred` or `unknown` on hours and cost. `known` only comes from fully parsed OSM tags or a hand checked official source with a date and URL. |
| DS-3 | optimizer | travel minutes between any two candidates | each day is physically possible | `travel_legs()` returns minutes and mode for every ordered pair. A leg is `walk` only within the traveler's `max_walk_minutes`, otherwise `transit`. No network call. |
| DS-4 | traveler | each sight to appear once | my plan never repeats a place | Around the richest record: same wikidata id within 1 km (100 m when the names differ) or same name within 60 m become one, with no chaining through neighbours. A venue never merges with a sight. Each city wall or aqueduct is one stop placed on one of its pieces. Copies of a curated sight are folded into it. |
| DS-5 | ranker | a popularity value for every place | famous sights rank apart from a corner cafe | Every ingested place has `popularity` in 0..1 built from OSM tag signals, documented as a proxy. |
| DS-6 | data engineer | to ingest any city by name | we can add a city without code changes | `make ingest CITY=...` writes a valid city folder. Raw responses are cached. Large cities work with `--bbox`. Overpass errors and timeouts are never cached as data. |
| DS-7 | team | measured numbers for data coverage and travel time error | design decisions rest on data, not guesses | `docs/data-spike.md` reports hours and price coverage for 4 cities. `docs/routing-spike.md` reports haversine error against OSRM foot routing. |
| DS-9 | traveler | a plan for any city I type, not only Rome and Lisbon | I can use the app wherever I travel | A city with no committed data is fetched live from OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, then one Overpass query for 5 km around the centre) and saved in `data/live/`, so only the first request waits. A saved city older than 30 days is fetched again; if OpenStreetMap is down then, the old copy is used. Only cities, towns and villages match, never a shop with the same name. A country or state over 100 km across, or a name OpenStreetMap does not know, gets a 422; a city-state such as Delhi or Mexico City plans. OpenStreetMap unreachable gets a 503 after three tries of the main server (5 s and 15 s apart, 120 s each at most). A city too dense for one query is fetched again for 2.5 km. The itinerary shows the city with its country, real place names and prices in the local currency. |
| DS-10 | traveler | a price and opening hours for every stop, even when OpenStreetMap has none | I can budget and time my day without "Price unknown" or "Hours unverified" | A place without a price gets a typical price for its kind in the local currency, marked `inferred` and shown with ≈; every country's currency has one (a rough exchange rate where there is no own row). Paid towers and observation decks (Empire State Building, Eiffel Tower) get a ticket estimate instead of "Free"; `fee=yes` without an amount gets a museum-ticket estimate. Hours that cannot be read become the usual hours for that kind of place, marked `inferred` ("Hours estimated"), and the planner follows them. Saved cities from before this change are filled in when they load. |
| DS-11 | traveler | the walk to and from the bus stop counted in my day | "0 min walking" never hides the walking a bus ride needs | Bus stops and stations come from OpenStreetMap with each live city. Every place gets its own walk to its nearest stop (straight line x 1.3 at 4.5 km/h, at most 15 min). A transit leg carries `walk_minutes` = walk to the first place's stop + walk from the stop nearest the next place, and the day's walking total includes it. Without stops (older saved cities) 3 + 3 minutes is used. |
| DS-12 | traveler | to know what each place is | I can decide whether a stop interests me | Famous places (OSM `wikidata` or `wikipedia` tag) get the first two sentences of their English Wikipedia article, credited "(Wikipedia)". Every other place gets a line from its OSM tags, e.g. "Restaurant serving indian and biryani food." Wikipedia being down never stops a city from loading. |
| DS-13 | traveler | each stop to say when it opens and closes | I can see why a stop is placed when it is | Each itinerary item carries `open_hours` for the visit's weekday; the website shows "Open 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM" next to "Hours verified" or "Hours estimated", and times as AM/PM. |
| DS-14 | traveler | a link to each place's official website, and each stop's location | I can check tickets and opening times myself, and see the day on a map | A place's OSM `website` (or `contact:website`) is kept in `descriptions.json`; only `http`/`https` links are kept and a bare domain gets `https://`. Each itinerary item carries `website` when there is one, plus `lat`/`lon` for the day map. The website shows "Website" only when there is no "Tickets" link to the same site. No phone numbers (the team chose links only). |
| DS-15 | traveler | to see each day's weather, with indoor stops preferred when rain is likely | I don't spend a rainy day walking between outdoor sights | Each day of a trip within the 16-day forecast carries `weather` (summary, rain chance, high and low). On a rainy day outdoor stops score lower (`RAIN_OUTDOOR_PENALTY` 0.35), so museums come first. The warning says "Rain is likely on {day}, so indoor stops were preferred that day" and names any outdoor stop that still had to stay, never claiming a stop "moved". A day with no forecast says "Forecast unavailable", never "Dry". With no forecast at all, the plan is exactly as before. A forecast service that is down never stops a plan. |
| DS-16 | team | a measured report of the data a traveler actually gets in many cities | we can show the data quality with numbers, not claims | `python -m ingest.quality "City" ...` loads each city the way the app does, plans a sample two-day trip and writes `docs/data-quality.md` and `.json`: hours and prices verified, estimated or unknown; stops described and from Wikipedia; the walk to the nearest stop. The summary line is worked out from the results: it names the cities with unknown hours or prices, if any. Each city says whether it was "downloaded now", "saved in the repo" or a "saved copy from an earlier fetch", decided by whether its data was written during the run, not by how long it took. One failing city never loses the others. |
| DS-17 | traveler | Wikipedia descriptions even in a big city | famous places are not left with a one-word line | When Wikipedia answers "slow down" (HTTP 429), the request waits as long as Wikipedia asks (`Retry-After`, at most 30 s) and tries again instead of giving up; batches to the same API are 1 s apart. (Paris went from 0% to 80% Wikipedia descriptions in the planned trip.) |
| DS-18 | traveler | the saved demo city, Rome, to show the same data as a live city | the demo shows descriptions, Wikipedia text, website links and real walks | Rome is rebuilt from fresh OpenStreetMap data with descriptions, websites and stop walks. `python -m ingest --wikipedia` asks Wikipedia for a saved city as live cities already do; it is off unless asked, and `make snapshot-rome` uses it. |
| DS-8 | traveler | the planner to know about rain and outdoor stops | asking what if it rains can move outdoor stops | Daily forecast for trip dates up to 16 days ahead, `None` further out, cached for 6 hours. `is_outdoor(place)` by primary category. |
